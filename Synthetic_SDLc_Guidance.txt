Document: Synthetic SDLC Guidance
Version: 1.0

Waterfall: suitable when requirements are stable and formal approvals dominate.

V-Model: suitable when verification and validation are strict and traceability is important.

Spiral: suitable when technical uncertainty and project risk are high.

Agile: suitable when requirements evolve frequently and stakeholder feedback is continuous.

DevSecOps: suitable when secure continuous integration/deployment and continuous security
validation are important.

Hybrid: suitable when a project combines strong regulatory/verification needs with evolving
requirements.
